CHUDAIL BUSTERS
a co-op horror for 1 to 6 aatma

A chudail: a woman with long black hair whose feet point backwards. She waits in an old sanatorium, a wedding haveli and a burnt-out school, and every night you wake up in one of them. She still walks the halls. She doesn't stop and she never sleeps.

Somewhere below the wards she drew a pentagram. It wants five gifts. The staff hid them all over the house and left riddles and marks on the walls to find them again. Fill the star, trap her in it, then get out. It hears you running. It sees your light. Shine your torch in its face and it slows down, but then it knows exactly where you are.
