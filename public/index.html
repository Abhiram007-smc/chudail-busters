CHUDAIL
a co-op horror for 1 to 6 souls

Ashgrove Sanatorium closed in 1974, the night the staff vanished. The patients whispered about a chudail: a woman with long black hair whose feet point backwards. She still walks the wards. She doesn't stop and she never sleeps.

Read the notes the staff left behind: they say where the bones are hidden. Find the five bone effigies to break the seal on the foyer door, then get out. It hears you running. It sees your light. Shine your torch in its face and it slows down, but then it knows exactly where you are.
